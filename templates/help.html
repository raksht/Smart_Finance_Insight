Help & Guide

A quick tour of what each part of Smart Finance Insights does.

The Dashboard gives you a daily snapshot: income, expenses, savings, and budget usage. The Intelligence page goes deeper - a Financial Health Score, AI-generated insights, budget recommendations, your savings trend, goal progress, and risk analysis, all in one place.

Set a monthly limit per category under Budget, and the app tracks how much you've spent against it. Bills lets you record due dates so you get reminders before (or after) they're due. Debts tracks loans and EMIs, which feeds your Debt-to-Income Ratio on the Intelligence page.

Add each holding under Investments. Asset Allocation shows how your money is split across asset classes, while Portfolio gives a fuller analytics view - growth over time, top/underperforming assets, and risk level.

The bell icon in the top bar shows live alerts - budget overspending, low balance, upcoming or overdue bills, goal deadlines, and investment gains/losses. Nothing is pre-stored; alerts are generated fresh from your current data every time you load a page.

Both live as icons in the top bar. Dark Mode switches the whole app's theme. Privacy Mode blurs amounts and headings - handy if you're screen-sharing or in a public space. Both preferences are remembered on this device.

Everything you enter is stored locally in this app's own database file. Passwords are hashed, never stored as plain text.
